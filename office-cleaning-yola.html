Office & Shop Cleaning in Yola

Cleaning contracts for offices, shops, clinics and schools across Jimeta and Yola Town, on a schedule that fits your opening hours.

Booking now for January 2027

A clean workplace, without managing it yourself

Customers and staff notice a clean shop floor, a fresh toilet and a tidy reception. We take that off your list with a regular cleaning contract and a clear checklist, so you know what gets done on every visit.

Choose how often

  • Daily: for busy shops, clinics and offices with lots of visitors
  • Several times a week: for most offices
  • Weekly or monthly: for smaller premises or a regular top-up
  • One-off: for a single deep clean, an inspection or an event

We work within our hours of 7:00am to 7:00pm, every day, so we can clean before you open or after you close.

What a contract can cover

  • Reception, offices, meeting rooms and shop floors
  • Desks, counters, shelves and display areas
  • Toilets and staff kitchens, cleaned and restocked with your supplies
  • Floors swept and mopped, entrances and walkways kept clear
  • Bins emptied and rubbish taken to your outside collection point
  • Glass doors, windows and display fronts

What you get with a contract

  • A written quote and checklist agreed before we start
  • A regular team who learns your premises
  • One contact for questions, changes and feedback
  • An easy way to add a periodic deep clean

Need a one-off reset first? Book a deep clean before your regular contract begins.

How booking works

  1. Ask for a quote with the form, on WhatsApp or by phone. Tell us the size of the space and when you'd like us.
  2. Confirm the details. We reply within one business day and confirm the price, the checklist and the time in writing.
  3. We clean at the agreed time, working through the agreed checklist.
  4. Check it with us. If something was missed, tell us within 48 hours and we'll put it right.

Questions about this service

Can you clean outside our opening hours?

Yes, within our hours of 7:00am to 7:00pm every day. Many businesses choose an early-morning or late-afternoon visit.

How is the price for a contract worked out?

It depends on the size of your premises, how often you need us and what the checklist covers. We visit or ask for details, then send a written quote.

Can we change the schedule later?

Yes. Tell us if your needs change and we will update the schedule and checklist with you.

Do you clean shops and clinics as well as offices?

Yes. We clean offices, shops, clinics, schools and other business premises.
