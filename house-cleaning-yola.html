House Cleaning in Yola

Regular or one-off cleaning for homes, flats and student rooms across Jimeta, Yola Town and nearby areas, so you can come home to a clean, fresh space.

A clean home without giving up your weekend

Between work, family and the dust that settles on everything in the dry season, keeping a house clean in Yola takes real time. Our house cleaning service takes care of the routine jobs, either on a regular schedule (weekly or every two weeks) or as a one-off visit when you need it.

Every visit follows a checklist we agree with you before we start, so you know exactly what will be done.

What's included in a standard house clean

  • Dusting surfaces, shelves, window sills, switches and door handles
  • Sweeping and mopping all floors, including under easy-to-move furniture
  • Kitchen: wiping counters, the outside of appliances, the sink and the stovetop
  • Bathrooms and toilets: cleaning the toilet, sink, shower or bath, mirrors and tiles
  • Making beds and tidying living areas
  • Emptying bins and taking rubbish to your outside bin

Need more than this? A deep clean goes further, with inside cupboards, appliances and hard-to-reach areas.

Who it suits

  • Families who want help with the weekly clean
  • Busy professionals who want to come home to a tidy house
  • Students sharing rooms or flats near campus
  • Landlords keeping furnished or short-let flats ready for guests

Supplies and equipment

We'll confirm in your quote whether we bring cleaning products and equipment or use yours. If anyone in the house has allergies or you prefer particular products, tell us when you book.

How booking works

  1. Ask for a quote with the form, on WhatsApp or by phone. Tell us the size of the space and when you'd like us.
  2. Confirm the details. We reply within one business day and confirm the price, the checklist and the time in writing.
  3. We clean at the agreed time, working through the agreed checklist.
  4. Check it with us. If something was missed, tell us within 48 hours and we'll put it right.

Questions about this service

How often can you clean my house?

As often as you need: one-off, weekly, every two weeks or monthly. We'll agree a regular day and time with you.

Do I need to be at home during the clean?

No. Many customers give us access and go about their day. We'll agree access arrangements with you when you book.

How much does house cleaning cost in Yola?

It depends on the size of your home and how often you want us. Ask for a free quote and we will confirm the price in writing before any work starts.

Do you clean student rooms and small flats?

Yes. We clean single rooms, self-contained flats and shared apartments. Let us know the number of rooms when you ask for a quote.
