Deep Cleaning in Yola

A top-to-bottom clean for homes and offices that need a proper reset, from the kitchen grease to the harmattan dust in every corner.

When a regular clean is not enough

A deep clean reaches the places a weekly clean doesn't: inside cupboards and appliances, behind furniture, along skirting boards and up on fans and light fittings. It's the right choice if your home hasn't been cleaned properly in a while, after an illness, before a celebration, or when the harmattan season has left a layer of dust everywhere.

What's included in a deep clean

Everything in a standard house clean, plus:

  • Inside kitchen cupboards and drawers (emptied by you or by us, as agreed)
  • Degreasing the stovetop, extractor hood and kitchen tiles
  • Inside the fridge, freezer and oven
  • Descaling taps, showers and tiles in bathrooms and toilets
  • Ceiling fans, light fittings, air-conditioner grilles and high shelves
  • Skirting boards, door frames, window frames and burglary-proof bars
  • Moving light furniture to clean underneath and behind it

Deep cleaning after harmattan

From December to February the harmattan wind brings fine dust into every room. A deep clean at the end of the season clears it from fans, frames, curtains and corners so it does not keep resettling. Our free harmattan cleaning guide has tips for keeping it under control between cleans.

For offices too

We deep clean offices, shops and clinics as well as homes. Many businesses book a deep clean once or twice a year alongside a regular office cleaning contract.

Questions about this service

What is the difference between a deep clean and a regular clean?

A regular clean keeps things tidy week to week. A deep clean covers the areas that build up over months, like inside appliances and cupboards, high fittings and behind furniture.

How long does a deep clean take?

It depends on the size and condition of the space. We will give you an estimate with your quote so you can plan your day.

How often should I book a deep clean?

Most homes benefit from one every few months, and especially at the end of the harmattan season. We can suggest a schedule when we see your space.

Do you deep clean before or after events?

Yes. Let us know the date of your event and we will schedule the clean around it.
