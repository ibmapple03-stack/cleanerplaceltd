FAQ

Common Questions

What areas does CleanerPlace service?

We currently serve communities across Abuja, including Maitama, Jahi, Katampe, Guzape, and Wuse, with residential, commercial, and municipal contracts. Contact us to confirm coverage at your address.

How is recyclable material processed?

All recyclables are sorted and processed at our eco-certified facility, with full traceability reporting provided to commercial and municipal partners.

Can I get a custom service plan for my business?

Yes — our commercial team builds tailored pickup frequency, container sizing, and reporting packages around your operation. Request a free quote to get started.

Do you offer community clean-up events?

We partner with schools, municipalities, and resident groups on seasonal clean-up drives and recycling education programs throughout the year.
